SHAREDOWNLOAD | CHAY KHONG CAN CAI PYTHON

Giai nen va giu nguyen ca thu muc, gom _internal. Mo tren Windows x64.

Demo mot may:
1. Mo ShareDownload-Demo.exe. Ba tab S1/S2/S3 co kho tach rieng.
2. Cho dong config.dat co xac nhan tu ca ba may.
3. Mo ShareDownload-Client.exe, tai config.dat vao downloads.

Ba may LAN:
1. Mo ShareDownload-Server.exe tren moi may va chon S1, S2 hoac S3.
2. Dung Ho so dia chi LAN de nhap/xuat IPv4 va cong; ap dung tren
   tung server. May C1 mo ShareDownload-Client.exe va nhap ho so.
3. Neu can dong bo: tao cum tren S1 voi IP/DNS LAN cua S1, xuat
   loi moi rieng cho S2/S3, roi nhap loi moi tren dung server.
4. Bat phuc vu cac server. Chi mo TCP cong tai tren LAN tin cay.
   Neu dong bo, chi S1 can mo them cong HTTPS dong bo (mac dinh 5100).
5. Them tep tren server va chon pham vi. C1 chon tep tu danh muc.

server-data chua du lieu va thong tin rieng. Khong phat tan lai
thu muc da chay server-data, loi moi rieng, hoac tep da tai.
Giu _internal de chay; khong sua tep ben trong _internal.
